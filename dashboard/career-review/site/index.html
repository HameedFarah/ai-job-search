Workflow, data and Gmail notes

Open a job: click anywhere on a card (not on buttons, selects or links) to open its detail overlay. The overlay shows score, dates, the selected resume template, cover letter, email text, stage controls, comments and the AI-request queue — all inside the dashboard, no page navigation. Esc closes it.

Add your own job: use Add job to paste a public vacancy link, paste the full job description, or both. Link metadata is extracted when available. The vacancy is deduplicated and scored through the normal Career Engine; eligible jobs are packaged immediately, while blocked jobs stay visible for review. Nothing is sent or submitted.

Move cards: drag between columns or use the stage select / action button on each card. Found and review cards move directly to Ready for review; use Process all ≥ N for actual Career Engine package processing. Stage, resume template, comments, AI requests and design preference are saved to the private site data and survive re-publishes.

Bulk edit: select jobs with the card or table checkboxes, then use the bulk status bar. Applied / sent remains an individual confirmation because opening a portal is not submission evidence. Switch between Kanban and Table under Display.

Gmail and application links: external actions open in separate tabs. A fresh Hermes scan also asks for a read-only reconciliation of replies and confirmations for jobs already marked applied/sent; nothing is sent automatically.

External gate: Ready for review is the final internal pre-submission state. You still submit or send manually, then confirm it here.

Recency tags: Fresh ≤2 days · Recent ≤7 days · Aging ≤30 days · Old >30 days · Unknown when no date. Score tags: High ≥85 · Good 70–84 · Marginal 55–69 · Low <55.
